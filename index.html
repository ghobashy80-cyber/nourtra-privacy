Nourtra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Nourtra is a fitness and nutrition app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information. It is written to match how the app is actually built — the engineering rules behind it are in the project's PRIVACY_AND_SAFETY document.

The short version

Your health and fitness data stays on your device. We do not have accounts, we do not have a copy of your data, and we cannot see your workouts, your food diary, your weight or anything from Apple Health. Three things leave your device, all described below: food search text; — only if you switch it on — a summary for the AI coach along with the messages you send it; and a pseudonymous per-device code used solely to protect the coach service from abuse.

What stays on your device, always

  • Your profile, goal, schedule, equipment and preferences
  • Your workouts and every logged set
  • Your food diary and saved meals
  • Your weight, measurements and any vitals read from Apple Health
  • Calculated figures: calorie targets, trends, readiness, recovery

These are stored in the app's own database on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and — where you have given permission — mirrored into Apple Health, which is your relationship with Apple, not with us. None of it is uploaded to us. There is no cloud backup of it on our side, because there is no account to attach it to.

What leaves your device

1. Food search text and barcodes. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, that search text or barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts and to a lookup service we operate that relays queries to the USDA food database. These requests carry no name, no account, no device identifier and no health information — only what you typed or scanned. We do not retain them.

2. The AI coach — only if you turn it on. The coach is off by default. Before it can be enabled you are shown a consent screen listing the exact fields that would be sent. If you enable it, the app sends a small set of derived numbers — for example your goal, how many sessions you completed this week, your calorie target, and body basics you typed in yourself — to our server, which relays them to Anthropic's Claude API to produce a reply. Messages you deliberately send the coach also travel: a question you type or dictate, and a program you paste to import. It never sends raw Apple Health records, your food diary, or your name. Nothing is retained on our server beyond the moment of the request. You can turn the coach off at any time, and the rest of the app works exactly the same without it.

3. A pseudonymous device code, for abuse protection only. Coach requests carry a one-way scrambled code derived from your device (it contains no personal information and cannot be reversed into anything about you). Our server uses it solely to limit how many requests each device can make per hour, keeping the service available. It is stored only as short-lived hourly counters, is never linked to your identity or health data, and is never used for tracking or advertising.

Voice stays on the phone. If you dictate to the coach, your speech is transcribed on this device only — the microphone audio never leaves your phone, and the feature simply does not appear on devices that cannot transcribe your language locally. The transcribed text is shown to you first; only what you choose to send is sent.

Content suggesting a medical emergency or an eating disorder is detected on your device and is not sent anywhere; the app shows guidance to seek professional help instead.

What we never do

  • We never sell or share your data.
  • We never use your health or fitness data for advertising or marketing.
  • There are no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking.
  • We never write invented or estimated measurements into Apple Health.

Apple Health

The app asks for Health permissions one feature at a time, with a plain-language explanation before each request, and you can grant any subset. Declining leaves the app fully usable — features that need data you have not shared simply stay hidden. You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in the Health app.

Your control over your data

- Export: Settings offers a full export of your app data as a JSON file. - Delete: you can delete individual records, or erase everything — which also removes the entries Nourtra wrote into Apple Health. - No account to close: because we hold nothing, there is nothing for you to ask us to delete.

Children

Nourtra is intended for adults. Onboarding asks you to confirm you are 18 or older.

This is not medical advice

Nourtra is a wellness app. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any disease or injury. Calorie burn, readiness and similar figures are estimates and are labelled as estimates in the app. Talk to a qualified professional before making significant changes to how you train or eat, and especially if you have any medical condition.

Egypt — Law 151/2020

Health data is sensitive personal data under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law. Because processing happens on your own device and we do not receive or store your health data, we do not hold it as a controller. The AI coach, when you enable it, makes us a momentary processor of the derived figures listed above — with your explicit in-app consent, with no identifiers attached, and with no retention.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the app will tell you before the change takes effect.
